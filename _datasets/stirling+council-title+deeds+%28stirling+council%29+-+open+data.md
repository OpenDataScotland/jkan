notes: '<p><strong>Overview</strong></p>

  <p>This Feature Service provides a reference dataset of Stirling Council''s land
  title deeds. It offers a spatial and descriptive view of properties held by the
  council, reflecting various title statuses and ownership categories. This data is
  intended for reference purposes only and does not represent a definitive or legally
  binding record of land ownership.</p>

  <p><strong>Feature Layer</strong></p>

  <ul><li><strong>Stirling Council Title Deeds</strong>: This layer details council-owned
  land parcels within the Stirling region, including fields for local authority codes
  and names, title status (e.g., Sold, Council Title, Buy-back, Long Lease, Part Interest,
  Unclassified), council account classification, and area measurements. The dataset
  captures the extent of these land holdings along with the processing date, providing
  insight into the council''s land asset management.</li></ul>

  <p><strong>Geographic Coverage</strong></p>

  <p>The data covers the Stirling Council area, providing detailed cadastral information
  pertinent to this jurisdiction.</p>

  <p><strong>Data Currency and Licensing</strong></p>

  <p>The dataset is updated tri-weekly to ensure currency and accuracy. It is made
  available under the UK Open Government Licence with specific attributions for data
  sources including Ordnance Survey and other national bodies. Users should note that
  the data is offered "as is" without warranties and should not be used as a sole
  authoritative source.</p>

  <p><strong>Potential Uses</strong></p>

  <ul><li>Property and asset management analysis by local government and affiliated
  agencies.</li><li>Community planning and development assessments requiring up-to-date
  council land ownership references.</li><li>Reduction of FOI requests and community
  enquiries by providing transparent open data access.</li></ul>'
