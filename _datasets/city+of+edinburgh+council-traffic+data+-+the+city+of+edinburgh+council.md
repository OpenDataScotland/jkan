notes: '<p>This is a summary of available traffic and transport data commissioned
  by the City of Edinburgh Council since 2016. Where-ever possible datasets seek to
  provide data for all non-motorised modes (pedestrians, cycles etc.) and motorised
  modes (bus, car, HGV etc.), but are project specific.</p>

  <p>All data uploaded to these pages are publicly available as part of the UK Open
  Government Licence (OGL).</p>

  <p>Data types uploaded here include: Junction Turn Counts (JTC), Automatic Traffic
  Counts (ATC), Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR)</p>

  <p>Statutory projects associated with these data: Low Emission Zone (LEZ) (2016
  - 2025) and Local Transport Strategy (LTS) (2016 - present). Latest LTS City Mobility
  Plan (CMP) (2021- 2030).</p>

  <p>Technical data related queries, please contact: "TransportStrategyandDevelopment@edinburgh.gov.uk".
  Full address: Transport Strategy &amp; Partnerships, Transport &amp; Mobility, Place,
  The City of Edinburgh Council. </p>'
