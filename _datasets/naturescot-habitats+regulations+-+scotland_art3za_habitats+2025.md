notes: '<p>This spreadsheet is published pursuant to the requirements of Regulation
  3ZA of The Conservation (Natural Habitats, &amp;c.) Regulations 1994 (as amended).
  It contains an evaluation of the conservation status of habitat types (features)
  listed on Annex I of the Habitats Directive (as transposed to the Habitats Regulations),
  the pressures acting on those features, and the conservation measures in place or
  required.<br/><br/>This file consists of one spreadsheet composed of 5 sheets (this
  includes an Explanatory Information sheet). These are:<br/><br/><em> Explanatory
  Information - Habitats Regulations 3ZA Report. Data for reporting period 2019-2024:
  Scotland - Habitats<br/></em> Report * containing information on: Range, Area of
  habitat, Structure and functions, Pressures, Conservation measures, Future prospects,
  Conclusions, UK National Site Network (pSCIs, SCIs, SACs) coverage for Annex I habitat
  types, and Complementary information.<br/><em> Pressures * containing details on
  pressures for each feature. Pressures are set out using a standard set of codes
  produced by the EU 2019-2024 Article 17 of the Habitats Directive reporting (the
  full list is presented in the Approach Document, linked below). Where there is more
  than one pressure applicable to a feature, there is more than one row in the sheet.
  <br/></em> Measures * containing details on conservation measures for each feature.
  Measures are set out using a standard set of codes produced by the EU 2019-2024
  Article 17 of the Habitats Directive reporting (the full list is presented in the
  Approach Document, linked below). Where there is more than one measure applicable
  to a feature, there is more than one row in the sheet. <br/><em> Audit Notes * containing
  further methodological detail on the information in the Report, Pressures, and Conservation
  measures sheets.<br/><br/>Further information on the methodology, including reporting
  guidelines and the Approach Document can be found on the JNCC website.<br/><br/></em>JNCC,
  DAERA, NatureScot, NE, NRW (2026). Habitats Regulations Reporting 2019-2024: Approach
  Document. Joint Nature Conservation Committee, Peterborough.*  Available to download
  from the <a href="https://jncc.gov.uk/our-work/habitats-regulations-reporting-2019-to-2024/">JNCC
  website</a>. <br/><br/>The Implementation Report has been produced in accordance
  with Regulation 3ZA of the Conservation (Natural Habitats &amp;c.) Regulations 1994
  (the Habitats Regulations). It gives an overview of the implementation of measures
  taken in Scotland to meet the provisions of the Birds and Habitats Directives, and
  to achieve the objectives of those Directives during the period 2019-2024. You can
  find the report on the <a href="https://www.gov.scot/isbn/9781806436545">Scottish
  Government website</a>.<br/><br/>This dataset will not be updated. The Habitats
  Regulations ensure that European sites are protected and managed and continue to
  operate, as they have done since their original designation. This includes reporting
  on the sites and the network every six years. Datasets associated with future reporting
  will be published separately as appropriate.</p>'
