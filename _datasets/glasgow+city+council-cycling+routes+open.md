notes: '<p style=''margin-left:36.0pt;''>This spatial dataset represents the existing
  cycle routes within Glasgow City. It has been updated in 2025 to streamline the
  data structure in line with the <i>Cycling by Design 2021</i> guidelines. A new
  standardized terminology has been introduced to improve clarity and enhance the
  user experience, as outlined below:</p>

  <ul><li><strong>Segregated Cycle Lane</strong>: A lane physically separated from
  motor traffic, either on-road, on the footway, or remote, using hard or soft segregation.</li><li><strong>Cycle
  Lane</strong>: A lane marked on the carriageway with a painted line, which may be
  advisory (dashed line) or mandatory (solid line).</li><li><strong>Shared Footway</strong>:
  A footway adjacent to a road with no segregation or demarcation between cyclists
  and pedestrians.</li><li><strong>Shared Path</strong>: A footpath remote or detached
  from the road with no segregation or demarcation between cyclists and pedestrians.</li><li><strong>Mixed
  Traffic Street</strong>: A low-speed, low-traffic environment where cycles and motor
  traffic share the road.</li></ul>

  <p style=''margin-left:36.0pt;''>For any queries or feedback regarding the dataset,
