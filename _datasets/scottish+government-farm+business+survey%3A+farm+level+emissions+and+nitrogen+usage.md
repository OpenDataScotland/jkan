notes: "<p>Farm level emissions and nitrogen usage data from the Scottish Farm Business\
  \ Survey.<br/><br/>The Scottish Farm Business Survey is an annual survey of around\
  \ 400 commercial-sized farms. Farms which are part-time, or with economic activity\
  \ below approximately \xA320,000 are not included. Farms that do not receive support,\
  \ such as pigs, poultry and horticulture, are not included in the survey.<br/><br/>Data\
  \ from the June Agricultural Census are used to weight the farm business income\
  \ estimates. The FBS is representative of around 11,000 farms in Scotland, which\
  \ is around 21% of all farms included in the June Agricultural Census. These farms\
  \ cover 64% of Scotland's agricultural land, employ 46% of those employed in Scottish\
  \ farming, and produce 95% of standard output from the farming sectors that are\
  \ included in the FBS.<br/><br/>On-farm greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions are estimated\
  \ using a life cycle assessment (LCA) based carbon calculator (<a href=\"https://www.agrecalc.com/\"\
  >Agrecalc</a>). They do not account for any sequestration by woodland areas or soil.\
  \ Nitrogen estimates are based on standard estimates of nitrogen content in all\
  \ farm inputs and outputs where possible. Nitrogen estimates are not made for organic\
  \ farms, which means a small proportion of the sample are excluded.<br/><br/>The\
  \ dataset contains the following variables:<br/><br/>Average farm absolute GHG emissions\
  \ per hectare <br/>Average farm emission intensity from GHG<br/>Average farm nitrogen\
  \ balance<br/>Average farm nitrogen use efficiency<br/><br/>The median, lower quartile\
  \ and upper quartile values for each variable are available for each of the years\
  \ 2019/20 to 2021/22, and for the following farm types:<br/><br/>Cereal <br/>General\
  \ Cropping <br/>Dairy <br/>Less favoured area (LFA) Livestock <br/>Mixed <br/>All\
  \ Types</p>"
