notes: '<p>Every year the Scottish Household Survey (SHS) asks adults whether they
  agree with the following statements about their involvement with people in their
  neighbourhood.<br/><br/><br/>\u2022 If I was alone and needed help, I could rely
  on one of my friends/relatives in this neighbourhood to help me <br/><br/>\u2022
  If my home was empty, I could count on one of my friend/relatives in this neighbourhood
  to keep an eye on my home <br/><br/>\u2022 I feel I could turn to friends/relatives
  in this neighbourhood for advice or support<br/><br/>\u2022 In an emergency, such
  as a flood, I would offer to help people in my neighbourhood who might not be able
  to cope well<br/><br/><br/><br/>The data is reported here on person level and is
  a combination of those who strongly agree and tend to agree with each statement.
  The data is available by:<br/><br/><br/>\u2022 Local Authority<br/><br/>\u2022 Gender
  of the randomly selected adult <br/><br/><em>In the 2018 Scottish Household Survey
  the gender question was revised. In previous years interviewers asked if household
  members were ''male'' or ''female''. In the 2018 survey the options given were:
  ''man/boy'', ''woman/girl'' and two additional responses: ''identified in another
  way'' and ''refused''.  In order to remain harmonised with other Statistics.Gov.Scot
  datasets, the previous response options ''male'' and ''female'' will be maintained.</em><br/><br/>\u2022
  Urban/Rural Classification<br/><br/>\u2022 SIMD quintiles<br/><br/>\u2022 Type Of
  Tenure<br/><br/>\u2022 Household Type<br/><br/>\u2022 Ethnicity of the randomly
  selected adult<br/><br/>\u2022 Walking distance to nearest greenspace<br/><br/>The
  <a href="http://www.gov.scot/Topics/Statistics/16002/Publications">most recent annual
  publication</a> and <a href="https://www2.gov.scot/Topics/Statistics/16002/PublicationQuestionnaire">full
  questionnaire</a> can be accessed on the Scottish Government website.<br/><br/>And
  the survey data for the full Scottish Household Survey is available from <a href="https://beta.ukdataservice.ac.uk/datacatalogue/series/series?id=2000048#!/abstract">the
  UK Data Service</a>.<br/><br/><br/></p>'
