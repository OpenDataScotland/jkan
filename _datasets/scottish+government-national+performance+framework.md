notes: <p>The National Performance Framework (NPF) is for all of Scotland. It aims
  to:<br/><br/><em> create a more successful country<br/></em> give opportunities
  to all people living in Scotland<br/><em> increase the wellbeing of people living
  in Scotland<br/></em> create sustainable and inclusive growth<br/>* reduce inequalities
  and give equal importance to economic, environmental and social progress<br/><br/>To
  help achieve its purpose, the framework sets out 'National Outcomes' which are measured
  using 'National Indicators'. The indicators provide a broad measure of national
  and societal wellbeing, incorporating a range of economic, social and environmental
  indicators and targets which are updated as soon as the data are available.<br/><br/><br/>This
  database contains all the data on indicators that comprise the National Performance
  Framework.  To find out more about the National Performance Framework, and the indicators
  that comprise it, please visit the <a href="https://nationalperformance.gov.scot/">National
  Performance Framework website.</a></p>
