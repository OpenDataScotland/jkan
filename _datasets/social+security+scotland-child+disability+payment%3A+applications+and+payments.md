notes: <p>Child Disability Payment is the replacement of the Disability Living Allowance
  for Children in Scotland, which was previously delivered by the Department for Work
  and Pensions. It is the first application-based disability benefit to be introduced
  by the Scottish Government and is administered by Social Security Scotland. It provides
  money to help with the extra care and mobility costs that a child living with a
  disability might have. <br/><br/>From the 26 July 2021, new applications were taken
  for Child Disability Payment for children under 16 that live in the pilot areas
  of Dundee City, Na h-Eileanan Siar and Perth and Kinross. On 22 November 2021, Child
  Disability Payment launched nationwide to all new applicants living in Scotland.
  The case transfer process, where individual ongoing awards of Disability Living
  Allowance for Children are replaced by an award of Child Disability Payment, began
  on 11 October 2021.<br/><br/>This dataset includes received applications, authorised
  applications and issued payments for Child Disability Payment. Statistics are provided
  for the last day of the month.<br/><br/>Further information on new applications
  and clients who have had their awards transferred, award types, processing times,
  reviews and re-determinations can be found in the associated <a href="https://www.socialsecurity.gov.scot/archived-publications">publication</a>.</p>
