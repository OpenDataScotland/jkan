notes: '<p><strong>Overview</strong></p>

  <p>This feature service presents a detailed spatial dataset showcasing the locations
  and management details of dog waste bins within Dundee City Council. The data shows
  the correct location of each bin on the map with &nbsp;supporting local efforts
  in urban cleanliness and environmental sustainability.</p>

  <p><strong>Feature Layer</strong></p>

  <ul><li><strong>Dog Bins</strong>: This layer provides comprehensive information
  on dog waste bin points across Dundee. It includes various types of bins classified
  by their material and mounting style, such as plastic or metal, and whether they
  are free standing or pole mounted. The dataset also outlines operational details
  like the collection route, frequency of bin servicing, and specific service days
  (Monday through Friday), facilitating efficient schedule planning and maintenance
  oversight.</li></ul>
