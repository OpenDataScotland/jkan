notes: "<div>A Housing Land Audit (HLA) is a key document in the planning process\
  \ that assesses the supply of land available for housing development within a local\
  \ authority area. It provides a detailed account of the housing sites that are expected\
  \ to contribute to the supply of new homes over a specific period, typically 5 to\
  \ 10 years.</div>\n<div><br /></div>\n<div><b>Purpose of a Housing Land Audit</b></div>\n\
  <div><ul><li>Monitoring Housing Supply: It tracks the progress of housing developments\
  \ and ensures that there is a sufficient pipeline of land to meet housing demand.</li><li>Planning\
  \ Policy Compliance: Helps local authorities and stakeholders assess whether housing\
  \ targets set in local development plans are being met.</li><li>Assessing Deliverability:\
  \ Identifies sites that are immediately available for development and those that\
  \ may require further planning, infrastructure, or land assembly before they can\
  \ be built on.</li><li>Supporting Decision-Making: Provides evidence for planning\
  \ decisions, appeals, and policy reviews.</li></ul></div>\n<div><b>Key Components\
  \ of a Housing Land Audit</b></div>\n<div><ul><li>Effective Housing Land Supply:\
  \ Sites that are expected to be developed within a set period (usually five years)\
  \ and are free of significant constraints.</li><li>Constrained Housing Land Supply:\
  \ Sites with constraints such as legal issues, lack of infrastructure, or landownership\
  \ problems, making them unlikely to be developed in the short term.</li><li>Completed\
  \ Housing Developments: Records the number of houses built within the audit period.</li><li>Future\
  \ Housing Land Supply: Includes sites allocated in development plans for housing\
  \ but not yet in the planning system.</li></ul></div>\n<div><b>Who Uses a Housing\
  \ Land Audit?</b></div>\n<div><ul><li>Local Authorities \u2013 To guide planning\
  \ policies and ensure sufficient land is available for housing.</li><li>Developers\
  \ &amp; Housebuilders \u2013 To understand the availability of land and plan future\
  \ housing developments.</li><li>Government &amp; Planning Inspectors \u2013 To assess\
  \ whether councils are meeting housing targets.</li><li>Communities &amp; Stakeholders\
  \ \u2013 To understand where new housing developments may be proposed in their area.</li></ul></div>"
