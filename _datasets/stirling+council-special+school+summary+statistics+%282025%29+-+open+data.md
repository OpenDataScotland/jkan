notes: '<p>Description based upon guidance and notes from the source publication.</p>

  <p><strong>Overview</strong></p>

  <p>This feature service provides school-level summary statistics for special schools
  in Scotland for the 2025 reporting period. It brings together pupil and teacher
  census information and supports a detailed view of school characteristics, staffing,
  enrolment, pupil composition, and free school meal registration patterns.</p>

  <p><strong>Content</strong></p>

  <ul><li><strong>School identification and reporting details</strong> including school
  codes, names, reporting period, release information, and source attribution.</li><li><strong>Staffing
  and enrolment measures</strong> such as teacher full-time equivalent, pupil roll,
  pupil-to-teacher ratio, and average class size.</li><li><strong>Pupil composition
  indicators</strong> covering gender, special school pupil counts, additional support
  needs, English as an additional language, Gaelic instruction, ethnicity, and free
  school meal registration.</li><li><strong>Contextual school profile fields</strong>
  describing school type, denomination, deprivation profile using Scottish Index of
  Multiple Deprivation quintiles, and urban-rural classification.</li></ul>

  <p><strong>Geographic Coverage</strong></p>

  <p>The table covers special schools in the Stirling Council area and is published
  by Stirling Council and the Scottish Government as open data.</p>

  <p><strong>Use Cases</strong></p>

  <ul><li>Monitoring special school enrolment and staffing levels</li><li>Supporting
  education planning and service analysis</li><li>Examining pupil composition and
  equity indicators</li><li>Comparing school characteristics across Scotland</li></ul>

  <p><strong>Licensing</strong></p>

  <p>The dataset is released under the Open Government Licence, with attribution required
  in line with the published licensing guidance.</p>'
