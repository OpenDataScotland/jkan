  to intentionally or recklessly damage the protected natural features of an SSSI.
  SSSIs were first designated under the National Parks and Access to the Countryside
  Act 1949. The majority of these were later re-notified under the Wildlife and Countryside
  Act 1981. All 1981 Act SSSI designations are carried forward, and all new SSSI designations
  are now made, under the Nature Conservation (Scotland) Act 2004.</p>'
