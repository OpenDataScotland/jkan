notes: <p>The data presented here can also be viewed on <a href="https://scotland.shinyapps.io/sg-scottish-health-survey/">an
  interactive dashboard.</a><br/><br/>The Scottish Health Survey (SHeS) has been carried
  out annually since 2008 and prior to this was carried out in 1995, 1998, and 2003.
  <br/><br/>Commissioned by the Scottish Government Health Directorates, the series
  provides regular information on aspects of the public's health and factors related
  to health which cannot be obtained from other sources. The SHeS series was designed
  to:<br/><br/>-estimate the prevalence of particular health conditions in Scotland<br/><br/>-estimate
  the prevalence of certain risk factors associated with these health conditions and
  to document the pattern of related health behaviours<br/><br/>-look at differences
  between regions and subgroups of the population in the extent of their having these
  particular health conditions or risk factors, and to make comparisons with other
  national statistics for Scotland and England<br/><br/>-monitor trends in the population's
  health over time<br/><br/>-make a major contribution to monitoring progress towards
  health targets.<br/><br/>Each survey in the series includes a set of core questions
  and measurements (height and weight and, if applicable, blood pressure, waist circumference,  and
  saliva samples), plus modules of questions on specific health conditions and health
  risk factors that vary from year to year. Each year the main sample has been augmented
  by an additional boosted sample for children. <br/><br/>More details on the Scottish
  Health Survey can be found on the <a href="https://www.gov.scot/collections/scottish-health-survey/">survey
  webpages on the Scottish Government website.</a></p>
