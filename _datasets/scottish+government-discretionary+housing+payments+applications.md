notes: <p>Discretionary Housing Payments (DHPs) are administered in Scotland by the
  32 Scottish Local Authorities. DHPs may be awarded when a Local Authority considers
  that a housing benefit or universal credit (including the housing element) claimant
  requires further financial assistance towards housing costs. DHPs can be used to
  provide support to claimants affected by some of the key welfare reforms, including
  the introduction of benefit cap, the Removal of the Spare Room Subsidy in the social
  rented sector ('RSRS', also known as the 'bedroom tax') and reductions in Local
  Housing Allowance.<br/><br/>As of April 2017, full responsibility for DHPs has been
  transferred to the Scottish Government and the Department for Work and Pensions
  no longer provides any funding. Scottish Government allocates funding for DHPs to
  each Local Authority, and Local Authorities are also free to provide additional
  funding from their own resources.<br/><br/>These <a href="https://www.gov.scot/collections/sg-social-security-scotland-stats-publications/#discretionaryhousingpaymentstatistics">annual
  DHP statistics</a> cover the period from 1 April to 31 March each financial year.<br/><br/>A
  determination is the decision taken on a Discretionary Housing Payment application
  to either make an award, or not to make an award.<br/></p>
