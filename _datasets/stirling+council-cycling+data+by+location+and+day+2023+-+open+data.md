notes: <p>Stirling Council currently have 25 sensors deployed in the local Stirling
  area and Balfron. These devices gather walking and cycling activity data.<div><br
  /></div><div>This dataset is a summary of activity, per day, across the entire year,
  for each of the measured locations. The following attributes are available within
  the table of data;</div><div><br /></div><div><ul><li>SiteID - unique device ID</li><li>Location
  - simple location description</li><li>Postcode - calculated postcode for the device
  location</li><li>DateStart - each day of data captured</li><li>Day, and Month -
  sorted into time-series order</li><li>Class - activity type</li><li>CountSum - sum
  of detected activity</li><li>PercentageChange - % change from previous day's CountSum
  (activities)</li><li>X, and Y - calculated coordinates of device.</li></ul><div>Please
  note; some devices have been moved/removed and therefore the data for each may not
  span the entire year. In addition, where a fault may have occurred, a device can
  return a range of nil activity.</div></div><div><br /></div><div>A table of data
  summarising activity trends by month is also available.</div></p>
