notes: '<p>This dataset contains the percentage of school leavers going to a ''positive
  destination'' on leaving school.  Positive destinations include: higher education,
  further education, training, voluntary work, employment, activity agreements.  More
  information on school leaver destinations is available <a href="https://www.gov.scot/collections/school-education-statistics/">here</a>.<br/><br/>Data
  is for senior phase (S4-S6) leavers from publicly funded secondary and grant aided
  secondary schools. This includes a small number of pupils who are on the roll of
  the school but attend full-time courses or further education outside of the school.<br/><br/>Data
  is not provided for Junior High Schools as these schools generally only take pupils
  as far as S4, after which they transfer to another centre to continue their education.
  <br/><br/>The positive leaver destination measure on Parentzone Scotland uses data
  from <a href="http://www.gov.scot/Topics/Education/Schools/curriculum/seniorphasebenchmarking">Insight</a>
  about school leavers, which is collected by Skills Development Scotland. Details
  about a leaver''s destination are only included in the measure if a link can be
  made between the leaver''s Skills Development Scotland destination record and a
  Pupil Census record with stage S4, S5 or S6 for the same academic year. In addition,
  the young person must not have a Pupil Census record in the following academic year
  in a senior phase stage (S4-S6).<br/><br/>For some data points, a special character
  is provided instead of a number. These have the meaning:<br/>   <br/><em> ''NA''
  - data not available<br/></em> ''<em>'' - the value has been hidden for disclosure
  reasons<br/></em> ''#'' - data not available because the school is a junior high
  school with no year S5 or S6</p>'
