  \ style='color:#2f2f2f; font-size:16px; font-weight:400; line-height:1.5; word-break:normal;'><p\
  \ style='margin:0;'>This report provides aggregated workforce information to support\
  \ transparency and public understanding of the Council\u2019s workforce, while protecting\
  \ employee confidentiality. It was developed in response to recurring Freedom of\
  \ Information (FOI) requests and, together with the associated datasets, aims to\
  \ make workforce information easier to access. It is intended for members of the\
  \ public, Council employees, researchers and others seeking information about the\
  \ Council\u2019s workforce.</p><p style='margin:0;'>&nbsp;</p><div style='color:#40566c;\
  \ font-size:16px; line-height:1.25; margin:0 0 14px;'><strong>Data Source and Outputs</strong></div><p\
  \ style='margin:0;'>Data is extracted monthly from the Council\u2019s Oracle Fusion\
  \ Human Resources system and processed through reporting systems to provide a workforce\
  \ snapshot at the date of extraction. The data is provided on the 8th of every month\
  \ and will be presented for publication within two working days. Given we are providing\
  \ a information based on an \"at time of extraction\" dataset, some errors may exist.\
  \ Adjustments and corrections will, as a matter of course, be reflected in subsequent\
  \ monthly reports.&nbsp;</p><p style='margin:0;'>&nbsp;</p><div style='color:#40566c;\
  \ font-size:16px; line-height:1.25; margin:0 0 14px;'><strong>Workforce Coverage</strong></div><p\
  \ style='margin:0;'>The report includes all current Council employees, including\
  \ teaching staff. It excludes casual workers, supply staff and elected members.\
  \ Employees remain included until their recorded termination date.</p><p style='margin:0;'>&nbsp;</p><div\
  \ style='color:#40566c; font-size:16px; line-height:1.25; margin:0 0 14px;'><strong>Terminology</strong></div><p\
  \ style='margin:0;'>Within this report we use the following terminology;</p><ul><li>Employees:\
  \ all persons currently employed by the council and included in this report at time\
  \ of extract.</li><li>Staff: all persons currently employed by the council, excluding\
  \ teachers, and included in this report at time of extract.</li><li>Teachers: all\
  \ teaching staff, and associated professionals, included in this report at time\
  \ of extract.</li><li>Headcount: count of all employees included in this report\
  \ at time of extract.</li><li>Full-time Equivalent: count of all paid postions within\
  \ the council (some employees may hold multiple paid positions) and included in\
  \ this report at time of extract.</li></ul></div></div>"
