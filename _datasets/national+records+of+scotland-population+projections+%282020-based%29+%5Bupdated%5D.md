  note that the Office for National Statistics (ONS) have produced new variant population
  projections using new international migration assumptions. They were published on
  27 January 2023. New assumptions were developed following the latest expert advice
  and latest provisional estimates of international migration.</em><br/><br/><em>ONS
  and NRS advise users to use these new <a href="https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/populationandmigration/populationprojections/datasets/2020basedinterimnationalpopulationprojectionsyearendingjune2022estimatedinternationalmigrationvariant">2020-based
  interim national population projections: estimated international migration variant</a>
  (which include figures for Scotland) instead of the <a href="https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/statistics-and-data/statistics/statistics-by-theme/population/population-projections/population-projections-scotland/2020-based/2020-based-unrevised">2020-based
  interim national population projections</a> published in January 2022. This is because
  this release includes more recent estimates of international migration in the averages
  used to calculate the short- and long-term international migration assumptions.</em><br/><br/><em>More
  information on the impact and key changes in the latest update (international migration
  variant) can be found on the <a href="https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/statistics-and-data/statistics/statistics-by-theme/population/population-projections/population-projections-scotland/2020-based">NRS
  website</a>.</em><br/><br/><br/>A projection is a calculation showing what happens
  under certain assumptions about future fertility, mortality and migration. The assumptions
  are based on past trends and do not take account of any future changes that may
  occur as a result of policy initiatives but may reflect the past impact of policy
  and economic changes. These projections are not, therefore, forecasts of what the
  government expects to happen based on policy.<br/><br/>The projections are based
  on mid-year population estimates and a set of underlying demographic assumptions
  regarding future fertility, mortality and migration. <br/><br/>For more information
  on population statistics, please see the NRS website here: <a href="https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/statistics-and-data/statistics/statistics-by-theme/population">https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/statistics-and-data/statistics/statistics-by-theme/population</a>.
  <br/><br/>Single year of age population projections data for Scotland are available
  on the <a href="https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/statistics-and-data/statistics/statistics-by-theme/population/population-projections/population-projections-scotland/2020-based/2020-based-unrevised">NRS
  website</a> for principal projections, and the <a href="https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/populationandmigration/populationprojections/bulletins/nationalpopulationprojections/2020basedinterim">ONS
  website</a> for the international migration variant.</p>'
