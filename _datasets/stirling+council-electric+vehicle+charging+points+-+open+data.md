notes: '<div><b>Electric Vehicle Charging</b></div>

  <div><br /></div>

  <div>To support electric car ownership and reduce pollution, we''ve installed electric
  vehicle charging points across the council area.</div>

  <div><br /></div>

  <div>As part of our Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure Policy, we have introduced:</div>

  <div><ul><li>charges for using these points</li><li>an overstay fee for rapid chargers</li><li>You
  can read about the decision to introduce these charges in the Stirling Council meeting
  minutes from 6 October 2022.</li></ul><div><br /></div></div>

  <div><b>Types of charger</b></div>

  <div><br /></div>

  <div>We provide 3 types of charger. What''s available will vary from one point to
  another. You can check this on the ChargePlace Scotland site.</div>

  <div><br /></div>

  <div><b>Standard charger</b></div>

  <div><ul><li>are ''alternating current'' (AC)</li><li>have a Type 2 connector</li><li>have
  a maximum power output of 7kW</li></ul></div>

  <div><b>Fast charger</b></div>

  <div><ul><li>are AC</li><li>have a Type 2 connector</li><li>have a maximum power
  output of 22kW</li></ul><b>Rapid charger</b></div>

  <div><ul><li>CCS connector</li><li>are ''direct current'' (DC)</li><li>have a maximum
  power output of 50kW</li></ul></div>

  <div><ul><li>CHAdeMO connector</li><li>are DC</li><li>have a maximum power output
  of 50kW</li></ul><ul><li>Type 2 connector</li><li>are AC</li><li>have a maximum
  power output of 43kW</li></ul><div>ChargePlace Scotland provides an interactive
  map and overview of all of Stirling Council''s chargers</div></div>'
