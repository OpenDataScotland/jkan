notes: '<p>Life expectancy (LE) is an estimate of how many years a person might be
  expected to live, whereas healthy life expectancy (HLE) is an estimate of how many
  years they might live in a ''healthy'' state. HLE is a key summary measure of a
  population''s health.<br/><br/>HLE is an estimate of how long the average person
  might be expected to live in a ''healthy'' state. Like LE, it is most often expressed
  for an entire lifetime from the time of birth. HLE at birth is the number of years
  that a new-born baby would live in good health if they experienced the death rates
  and levels of general health of the local population at the time of their birth,
  throughout their life.<br/><br/>HLE is calculated by combining LE and a measure
  of ''healthy'' health: in these HLE analyses for Scotland the measure used is self-assessed
  general health. This is self-reported by survey or Census respondents but has been
  shown to reflect both mental and physical health.<br/><br/>You can access further
  information about Life Expectancy and Healthy Life Expectancy statistics by visiting
  the relevant pages on <a href="https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/statistics-and-data/statistics/statistics-by-theme/life-expectancy">the  NRS
  website.</a></p>'
