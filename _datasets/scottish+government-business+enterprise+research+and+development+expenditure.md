notes: <p>The data in this series are sourced from the Business Enterprise Research
  and Development (BERD) survey conducted by the Office for National Statistics (ONS).  <br/><br/>We
  have put a pause on the updating of this series until more work can be carried out
  to quality assure the underlying source data.<br/><br/>Business Enterprise Research
  and Development (BERD) is measured by the expenditure on R&amp;D carried out by
  the business. This is regarded as a more accurate measure of R&amp;D than the funding
  received by a business for R&amp;D work, as not all of these funds received may
  be used for R&amp;D. This publication reports on R&amp;D expenditure in businesses
  in Scotland irrespective of the country of residence of the ultimate owner or users
  of the R&amp;D produced.<br/><br/>The BERD survey follows the definition of research
  and development proposed by the Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development
  (OECD) and published in the 'Frascati' Manual. According to the manual, R&amp;D
  is defined as:<br/><br/>'creative and systematic work undertaken in order to increase
  the stock of knowledge - including knowledge of humankind, culture and society -
  and to devise new applications of available knowledge'.  <br/><br/>R&amp;D must
  contain an appreciable amount of novelty.<br/><br/>Scotland's Business Enterprise
  Research and Development (BERD) is taken from the annual Scottish Government (SG)
  BERD publication. The data in this release are sourced from the Business Enterprise
  Research and Development (BERD) survey conducted by the Office for National Statistics
  (ONS). The Office for National Statistics (ONS) has been leading a redevelopment
  project for R&amp;D statistics. The first phase of the project concluded with the
  publication of the latest ONS BERD estimates, which reflect significant methodological
  changes. There is currently uncertainty over the robustness of the detailed breakdowns
  of the new BERD estimates, which includes the data for Scotland. The ONS have put
  a temporary pause on the National Statistics status of the detailed breakdowns of
  BERD until the second stage of the redevelopment project completes in 2023. The
  National Statistics status of the Scottish Government BERD publication has similarly
  been put on pause.<br/><br/>More detailed information can be found in our <a href="https://www.gov.scot/publications/business-enterprise-research-and-development-2021/">BERD
  Scotland 2021 publication</a> and in the <a href="https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/governmentpublicsectorandtaxes/researchanddevelopmentexpenditure/bulletins/businessenterpriseresearchanddevelopment/2021">ONS
  BERD UK 2021 publication</a>.</p>
