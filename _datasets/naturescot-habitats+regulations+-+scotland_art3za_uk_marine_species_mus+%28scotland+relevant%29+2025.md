notes: "<p>This spreadsheet is published pursuant to the requirements of the Habitats\
  \ Regulations (as amended)<em>. It contains an evaluation of the conservation status\
  \ of marine species (features) listed on Annexes II, IV and/or V of the Habitats\
  \ Directive (as transposed to the Habitats Regulations), the pressures acting on\
  \ those features, and the conservation measures in place or required. It also contains\
  \ draft conclusions at the Management Unit (MU) level for S1351 Harbour Porpoise\
  \ and S1349 Bottlenose Dolphin. The guidance has been applied in the same way as\
  \ for the UK-level assessments. <br/><br/>NOTE: that only data for species management\
  \ units relevant to Scotland have been included; data for the Offshore Channel and\
  \ South West England and Coastal West Channel have therefore been excluded. <br/><br/><br/>This\
  \ file consists of four spreadsheets. These are:<br/><br/></em> Report \xE2\u20AC\
  \u201C containing information on: Range, Population, Habitat for Species, Pressures,\
  \ Conservation measures, Future prospects, Conclusions, UK National Site Network\
  \ (pSCIs, SCIs, SACs) coverage for Annex II species, and Complementary information.<br/><em>\
  \ Pressures \xE2\u20AC\u201C containing details on pressures for each feature. Pressures\
  \ are set out using a standard set of codes produced by the EU 2019-2024 Article\
  \ 17 of the Habitats Directive reporting (the full list is presented in the Approach\
  \ Document, linked below). Where there is more than one pressure applicable to a\
  \ feature, there is more than one row in the sheet. <br/></em> Measures \xE2\u20AC\
  \u201C containing details on conservation measures for each feature. Measures are\
  \ set out using a standard set of codes produced by the EU 2019-2024 Article 17\
  \ of the Habitats Directive reporting (the full list is presented in the Approach\
  \ Document, linked below). Where there is more than one measure applicable to a\
  \ feature, there is more than one row in the sheet. <br/><em> Audit Notes \xE2\u20AC\
  \u201C containing further methodological detail on the information in the Report,\
  \ Pressures, and Conservation measures sheets.<br/><br/>Further information on the\
  \ methodology, including reporting guidelines and the Approach Document can be found\
  \ on the JNCC website: <br/><br/></em>JNCC, DAERA, NatureScot, NE, NRW (2026). Habitats\
  \ Regulations Reporting 2019-2024: Approach Document. Joint Nature Conservation\
  \ Committee, Peterborough.*  Available to download from the <a href=\"https://jncc.gov.uk/our-work/habitats-regulations-reporting-2019-to-2024/\"\
  >JNCC website</a>. <br/><br/>The Implementation Report has been produced in accordance\
  \ with Regulation 3ZA of the Conservation (Natural Habitats &amp;c.) Regulations\
  \ 1994 (the Habitats Regulations). It gives an overview of the implementation of\
  \ measures taken in Scotland to meet the provisions of the Birds and Habitats Directives,\
  \ and to achieve the objectives of those Directives during the period 2019-2024.\
  \ You can find the report on the <a href=\"https://www.gov.scot/isbn/9781806436545\"\
  >Scottish Government website</a>.<br/><br/>This dataset will not be updated. The\
  \ Habitats Regulations ensure that European sites are protected and managed and\
  \ continue to operate, as they have done since their original designation. This\
  \ includes reporting on the sites and the network every six years. Datasets associated\
  \ with future reporting will be published separately as appropriate.</p>"
