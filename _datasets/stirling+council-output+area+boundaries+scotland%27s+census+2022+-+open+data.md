notes: "<div><b>output area boundaries scotland's census 2022 - open data</b></div>\n\
  <div><br /></div>\n<div>This dataset shows the 2022 Census Output Areas (OAs). OAs\
  \ are the smallest geographical unit for which Census data is released, and as a\
  \ result, they act as the basic \u201Cbuilding-blocks\u201D for the creation of\
  \ other statistical geographies, e.g. Data Zones. The OAs are constructed by aggregating\
  \ together a small number of Postcodes. Because the OAs cover small areas and contain\
  \ relatively small numbers of households and population (households in the range\
  \ 25 to 89; population &gt;= 60), there is only a limited amount of Census data\
  \ that can be released without infringing confidentiality.</div>\n<div><br /></div>\n\
  <div>An important requirement during the creation of the 2022 OAs was to attempt\
  \ to keep the boundaries the same, or similar, as the 2011 OAs \u2013 this would\
  \ make it easier to compare over the time period</div>\n<div><br /></div>\n<div><div><b>Schema;</b></div><div><ul><li>Local\
  \ Authority Code</li><li>Local Authority</li><li>Output Area Code</li><li>Master\
  \ Postcode</li><li>Easting (X)</li><li>Northing (Y)</li></ul></div></div>"
