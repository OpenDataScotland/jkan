notes: "<div>Hands Up Scotland Survey 2023: Overview An Official Statistics Publication\
  \ for Scotland \u2013 May 2024</div>\n<div><br /></div>\n<div>The annual Hands Up\
  \ Scotland Survey, now in its fifteenth year, is published by Sustrans. The survey\
  \ is funded by Transport Scotland and is designed to provide reliable and up to\
  \ date information on mode of travel to school in Scotland.</div>\n<div><br /></div>\n\
  <div>The question posed to all school pupils and nursery children is, \u2018How\
  \ do you normally travel to school?\u2019 with a choice of travel modes.</div>\n\
  <div>The travel modes are categorised as follows:</div>\n<div><ul><li>Active travel:\
  \ walking, cycling and scootering or skating</li><li>Public sustainable travel:\
  \ bus</li><li>Multi-mode travel: park and stride (driven part of the way by car\
  \ and walk the rest)</li><li>Private motorised travel: driven (car) and taxi.</li></ul></div>\n\
  <div>Over three-quarters (78.9%) of all state schools in Scotland (excluding nurseries)\
  \ took part in the 2023 survey. Responses were received from over 443,000 school\
  \ pupils and over 39,000 nursery children. A full report detailing the national\
  \ level findings and greater technical detail on the data collection is also available.</div>\n\
  <div><br /></div>\n<div><a href='https://www.sustrans.org.uk/media/13733/hands-up-scotland-survey-2023_national-summary-report.pdf'\
  \ target='_blank' rel='nofollow ugc noopener noreferrer'>Document Link</a></div>\n\
  <div><br /></div>\n<div><b>Key findings</b></div>\n<div>Active travel to school\
  \ has declined since 2022, but remains higher than the pre-pandemic levels of 2019.</div>\n\
  <div>The key findings from the 2023 survey are:</div>\n<div><ul><li>In 2023, the\
  \ percentage of school pupils travelling actively to school, either by walking,\
  \ cycling, scootering or skating, is at 49.3%. This represents a continued decrease\
  \ since a 51.2% high in 2020, but is still higher than the pre-pandemic levels of\
  \ 2019 (47.8%)</li><li>Cycling is at its highest level of the past ten survey years\
  \ at 4.7%, 0.8 percentage points (pp) higher than the previous year</li><li>Walking\
  \ rates decreased for the third year in a row, decreasing by 1.3pp, but is still\
  \ by far the most common way school pupils get to school</li><li>The percentage\
  \ of pupils scootering or skating to school increased to 3.3%, its highest level\
  \ of the past ten survey years</li><li>Bus use has increased for the third year\
  \ in a row, increasing by 0.6pp to 16.4%. This is comparable to pre-pandemic levels\
  \ and is the highest level since 2017</li><li>The proportion of pupils being driven\
  \ to school saw a 0.6pp drop since 2022, to 22.5%. This is the lowest level since\
  \ 2016.</li></ul></div>"
