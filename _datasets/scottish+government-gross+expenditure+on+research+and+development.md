notes: '<p>Information on Scottish Gross Expenditure on Research and Development (GERD)
  and estimates of the four components of GERD: Business Enterprise (BERD), Higher
  Education (HERD), Government, which includes Research Councils (GovERD) and Private
  Non-Profit (PNP). <br/><br/>We have put a pause on the updating of this series until
  more work can be carried out to quality assure the underlying source data. <br/>
  <br/>Scotland''s Gross Expenditure on Research and Development (GERD) is taken from
  the annual Scottish Government (SG) GERD publication. GERD includes R&amp;D undertaken
  by business enterprise (BERD).  The Office for National Statistics (ONS) are leading
  a large-scale project to transform R&amp;D statistics, which is still in progress.
  This includes an improved methodology for the estimation of BERD spend, which better
  accounts for small businesses.  Further information on this was published by the
  ONS on 29 September 2022: <a href="https://www.ons.gov.uk/releases/comparisonofhmrcresearchanddevelopmenttaxcreditstatisticstoonsbusinessenterpriseresearchanddevelopmentstatistics">Comparison
  of ONS BERD with HMRC R&amp;D tax credit statistics</a> and 22 November 2022: <a
  href="https://www.ons.gov.uk/businessindustryandtrade/business/businessinnovation/articles/optionsfortransformationofbusinessenterpriseresearchanddevelopmentstatistics/2022-11-17">Options
  for Transformation of BERD Statistics</a>.<br/><br/>Due to these methodological
  changes, there is currently uncertainty over the robustness of the detailed breakdowns
  of the new BERD estimates, which includes the data for Scotland.  More work is underway
  to further improve how the BERD statistics are compiled in the future, including
  the development of new sampling methods.  The ONS have put a temporary pause on
  the National Statistics status of the detailed breakdowns of BERD until the methodological
  work is complete and there is more certainty on the quality of the sub-UK BERD data,
  and hence sub-UK GERD data.  The National Statistics status of the Scottish Government
  (SG) GERD publication has similarly been put on pause.  More detail is available
  in the <a href="https://www.gov.scot/publications/gross-expenditure-on-research-and-development-scotland-2020/">GERD
  Scotland 2020 publication</a>, in the <a href="https://www.gov.scot/publications/gross-expenditure-on-research-and-development-scotland-2021/">GERD
  Scotland 2021 publication</a>,  and in the latest <a href="https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/governmentpublicsectorandtaxes/researchanddevelopmentexpenditure/bulletins/ukgrossdomesticexpenditureonresearchanddevelopment/2021#measuring-the-data">ONS
  GERD UK publication</a>.<br/></p>'
