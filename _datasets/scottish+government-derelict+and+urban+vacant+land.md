notes: '<p>Scottish Vacant and Derelict Land Survey. <br/>Source: <a href="http://www.scotland.gov.uk/Topics/Statistics/Browse/Planning/Publications">http://www.scotland.gov.uk/Topics/Statistics/Browse/Planning/Publications</a><br/><br/>Derelict
  land (and buildings) is land which has been so damaged by development, that it is
  incapable of development for beneficial use without rehabilitation. In addition
  the land must currently not be used for the purpose for which it is held or a use
  acceptable in the local plan. Land also qualifies as derelict if it has an un-remedied
  previous use which could constrain future development.<br/><br/>Vacant land is land
  which is unused for the purposes for which it is held and is viewed as an appropriate
  site for development. This land must either have had prior development on it or
  preparatory work has taken place in anticipation of future development. This indicator
  covers vacant land (referred to as ''urban vacant'') that is located within settlements
  of over 2,000 in population as defined in council local plans.<br/><br/>Sites covering
  less than 0.1 hectare are excluded. The survey is voluntary. If a local authority
  does not complete the survey the most recent available data is used.<br/><br/>Due
  to the impacts of the COVID-19 Pandemic on the collection of SVDL survey for 2021
  the publication date remains under review and cannot at the present time be pre-announced.  If
  you have any questions please contact planstats@gov.uk.<br/></p>'
