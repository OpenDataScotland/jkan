notes: <p>The Scottish Contact Survey (SCS) is a behavioural survey, asking questions
  to panels of people who have been recruited to be broadly representative of the
  Scottish population. It was launched in August 2020 and is based on the <a href="https://cmmid.github.io/topics/covid19/comix-reports.html">CoMix</a>
  study, developed by the London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine (LSHTM).
  <br/><br/>Adults record their direct, face-to-face contacts made on the previous
  day, specifying certain characteristics for each contact including the age and sex
  of the contact, whether a meeting involved physical contact with another person,
  and where contact occurred (e.g. home, at work, while undertaking other activities,
  etc.). The average number of contacts each person has is calculated for a range
  of settings (home, work, school, and other). Exact ages of contacts is not recorded,
  so a sample weighted by the population between the minimum and maximum age reported
  for the contact is used. Further information and the code used to analyse the survey
  data are available on <a href="https://github.com/DataScienceScotland/scottish-contact-survey">GitHub</a>.
  SCS results have been routinely published in the <a href="https://www.gov.scot/collections/coronavirus-covid-19-modelling-the-epidemic/">Modelling
  the Epidemic</a> series of reports.<br/><br/>This data release contains information
  on the average number of contacts between age groups. Before 31 March 2022, the
  survey was run weekly using two panels of participants in alternative weeks and
  is reported as a rolling sample. The dates reported correspond to the midpoint of
  the survey period. From 31 March 2022, the two survey panels were merged and the
  survey frequency was reduced to fortnightly. The dates reported correspond to the
  first day of the survey period.<br/><br/>The Scottish Contact Survey closed in December
  2022 and, therefore, this dataset will no longer be updated.</p>
