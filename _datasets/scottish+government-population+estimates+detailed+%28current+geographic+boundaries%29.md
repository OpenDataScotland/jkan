notes: <p>Annual publication of mid-year population estimates by age and sex as at
  30 June every year for 2011 Data Zones. <br/><br/><a href="http://statistics.gov.scot/data/population-estimates-historical-geographic-boundaries">Population
  estimates based on 2001 data zones</a> are also available, but are no longer updated.
  <br/><br/>Data zones are the small area geography used by the Scottish Government
  to allow statistics to be available across a number of policy areas. Data zones
  were initially set up to nest within council area boundaries and to have a population
  of between 500 and 1,000 household residents. As much as possible, data zones were
  set up to contain households with similar social characteristics and to take into
  consideration physical boundaries. More information on data zone geography can be
  found on the <a href="http://www.gov.scot/Publications/2004/02/18917/33243">Scottish
  Government</a> website. There are 6,976 data zones in Scotland. <br/><br/>Following
  the 2011 Census the Scottish Government completed a consultation on the redrawing
  of data zone boundary and finalised the new data zones. 2011 Data Zones were published
  at the end of 2014.<br/><br/>Population for higher geographies can be aggregated
  from the population estimates for 2011 Data Zones.<br/><br/>This data is also available
  by single year of age on the NRS website.  These breakdowns are available for <a
  href="https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/statistics-and-data/statistics/statistics-by-theme/population/population-estimates/mid-year-population-estimates">Scotland
  and its council areas</a>, as well as for <a href="https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/statistics-and-data/statistics/statistics-by-theme/population/population-estimates/2011-based-special-area-population-estimates/small-area-population-estimates">2011
  data zones</a> and <a href="https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/statistics-and-data/statistics/statistics-by-theme/population/population-estimates/2011-based-special-area-population-estimates">other
  geographies</a>.<br/></p>
