notes: '<p><strong>Overview</strong></p>

  <p>This Feature Service provides detailed spatial information about public play
  areas and recreational facilities located within Dundee City Council''s administrative
  boundaries in Scotland. It is designed to support management, planning, and public
  accessibility of various types of outdoor recreational sites.</p>

  <p><strong>Feature Layers</strong></p>

  <ul><li><strong>Play Space</strong>: This layer represents individual recreational
  sites such as playgrounds, multi-use games areas, sports facilities, agility trails
  and outdoor fitness gyms. It includes key attributes like the names and addresses
  of play areas, the types of facilities available, and client details responsible
  for management or maintenance. The layer also highlights accessibility features
  including accessible play equipment and paths, parking availability, and administrative
  ward classifications within the city. Additional attributes track operational status,
  remarks, and update history to assist in effective facility monitoring and strategic
  planning.</li></ul>

  <p><strong>Geographic Coverage</strong></p>

  <p>The dataset focuses exclusively on the Dundee City Council area, offering a comprehensive
  spatial overview of recreational and play amenities distributed across multiple
  wards within the city.</p>

  <p><strong>Use Cases</strong></p>

  <ul><li>Enables local government and parks management teams to monitor, plan, and
  maintain public recreational facilities effectively.</li><li>Supports community
  members and visitors in identifying accessible and well-equipped play spaces throughout
  Dundee City.</li><li>Facilitates informed decision-making regarding facility upgrades,
  accessibility improvements, and maintenance scheduling.</li></ul>'
