  by mapping the distribution and operational details of dog waste disposal infrastructure
  throughout Dundee.</p><b>Feature Layer</b><ul><li><b>Dog Bins</b>: This layer includes

  or metal and by mounting style (free standing or pole mounted). It details collection
  routes, servicing frequency, and specific collection days (Monday to Friday), enabling
  efficient maintenance planning and operational monitoring.</li></ul><b>Geographic
  Coverage</b><p>The dataset covers the entire Dundee City Council area, providing
  an asset management tool for local environmental services and neighbourhood teams.</p><b>Applications</b><ul><li>Optimising
