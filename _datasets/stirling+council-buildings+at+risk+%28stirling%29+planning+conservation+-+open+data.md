  <div><br /></div>

  <div>The Buildings at Risk Register (BARR) brings together potential restorers and
  redevelopers with buildings and sites that are considered at risk or under threat.</div>

  <div><br /></div>

  <div>The historic places on the Register are all of architectural or historic importance.
  They are usually listed buildings or an unlisted building within a conservation
  area.</div>

  <div><br /></div>

  <div>A building at risk may be:</div>

  <div><ul><li>long-term vacant</li><li>neglected and/or poorly maintained</li><li>structurally
  unsound</li><li>damaged by fire</li><li>unsecured and open to the elements</li><li>threatened
  with demolition</li></ul></div>'
