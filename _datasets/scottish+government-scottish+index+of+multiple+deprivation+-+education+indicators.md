  Index of Multiple Deprivation (SIMD)</a>  is a tool for identifying the places in
  Scotland where people are experiencing disadvantage across different aspects of
  their lives. SIMD ranks 6,976 small areas, or data zones, covering the whole of
  Scotland from the most deprived to the least deprived and reports statistics on
  income, employment, health, education, access to services, crime and housing. This
  dataset contains indicators used to calculate SIMD 2016 and SIMD 2020 education
  domains. These indicators include:<br/><br/><em> School pupil attendance<br/></em>
  Attainment of school leavers<br/><em> Working age people with no qualifications:
  standardised ratio<br/></em> 17-21 year olds enrolling in higher education<br/>*
  16-19 year olds not participating in education, employment or training (this replaces
  the NEET indicator used in SIMD 2016)<br/> <br/>More information can be found on
  the Scottish Government webpages:<br/><a href="https://www2.gov.scot/Resource/0050/00504822.pdf">SIMD
  2016</a> and <a href="https://www.gov.scot/SIMD">SIMD 2020</a></p>'
