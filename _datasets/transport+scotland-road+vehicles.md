notes: <p>Figures for the total number of vehicles on Scottish roads, household car
  ownership (from the Scottish Household Survey) and total petrol and diesel consumed.
  <br/><br/>The number of vehicles on Scottish roads is taken from the Vehicle Information
  Database held by the Department for Transport, annual results from which are published
  in <a href="https://www.transport.gov.scot/publications/?publicationtype=1271">Scottish
  Transport Statistics</a>.<br/><br/>Estimates from the Scottish Household Survey
  are updated annually in Transport and Travel in Scotland. <br/><br/>SHS estimates
  are National Statistics and provide figures which are representative of households
  and individuals in Scotland annually. The SHS has an annual sample size of around
  10,000 individuals and, since 2012, has operated with a fully unclustered sample
  design. <br/>Due to the Covid pandemic there was a smaller sample size for the SHS
  survey in 2020.  We are therefore not be able to provide 2020 data broken down in
  as many ways as usual. For example, we are not able to provide data for individual
  local authorities.<br/><br/>Petrol and diesel consumption figures are estimated
  by AEA Energy and Environment (commissioned by the Department for Energy and Climate
  Change) and published in Scottish Transport Statistics annually. These estimates
  are produced from a range of data, including data from roadside monitoring equipment,
  average fuel consumption factors and the DfT's information about traffic flows on
  the road network.<br/><br/><br/></p>
