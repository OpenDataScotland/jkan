  \ in 2014, it was the first authoritative inventory of our native woods, and created\
  \ a baseline for future monitoring of change.</div>\n<div><br /></div>\n<div>The\
  \ total area of native woods surveyed was 311,153 hectares (ha) which was 22.5%\
  \ of woodlands or 4% of the Scottish land area.\_</div>\n<div><br /></div>\n<div>There\
  \ were also 13,383 ha of nearly-native woods (those with 40 to 50% native species\
  \ in the canopy).</div>\n<div><br /></div>\n<div>The most widespread threat to the\
  \ condition of native woods is excessive herbivore impact, largely due to deer.\_\
  \ Threats from non-native tree species and invasive non-native shrub species (especially\
  \ rhododendron) are locally important.</div>"
