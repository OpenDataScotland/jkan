  in Scotland on the 16 September 2019. Applications were taken from this same day.<br/><br/>This
  dataset includes received applications, authorised applications and issued payments
  for the Funeral Support Payment benefit. Statistics are provided for the last day
  of the month (e.g. figures for 2019-09 are for applications received or authorised
  as of 30 September 2019). <br/><br/>Further information on application, processing
