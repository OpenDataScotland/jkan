  the table of data;</div>

  and Month</li><li>Activity Type</li><li>Total - number of passing counts</li><li>Days
  Returned - number of days the sensor sent back a packet of data.</li><li>X, and
  Y - calculated coordinates of device.</li></ul><div>Please note; some devices have
  been moved/removed and therefore the data for each may not span the entire year.
  In addition, where a fault may have occurred, a device can return a range of nil
  activity.</div></div>'
