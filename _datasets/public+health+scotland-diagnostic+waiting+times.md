notes: <p>"These datasets report key statistics on diagnostic waiting times as a monthly
  summary of patients waiting times for eight key diagnostic tests,  non-key test
  investigations, and the number of tests performed for the eight key diagnostic tests.\r\n\r\nFrom
  November 2026 this publication will be updated on a monthly basis rather than a
  quarterly.\r\n\r\nAll publications and supporting material to this topic area can
  be found on the Public Health Scotland website (https://publichealthscotland.scot/publications/nhs-waiting-times-diagnostics/).
  A selection of information from this publication is included in NHS Performs (http://www.nhsperforms.scot/).\r\n\r\nNHS
  Ayrshire and Arran implemented the new waiting times calculations as detailed in
  the guidance issued December 2023 (https://www.gov.scot/publications/nhsscotland-waiting-times-guidance-november-2023/)
  into their patient management system (PMS) on 01 October 2025. Therefore, this is
  the first open data release to reflect the new waiting times calculations for Endoscopy
  (and Clinical Physiology tests which come under the heading of \u2018non-key tests')
  for this board. Note that Radiology statistics have always reflected waiting times
  adjustments in line with the 2023 guidance. There is no change to the number of
  waits that NHS Ayrshire and Arran report as a result of the 2023 Waiting Times Guidance
  implementation in October 2025. It is estimated that around 5.6% of wait lengths
  for this board are affected for Endoscopy, meaning that they are likely to be shorter
  under the new guidance. This is approximately 0.4% of Endoscopy waits and 0.1% of
  waits associated with the eight key diagnostic tests across NHSScotland. Full details
  can be found in the main report (https://publichealthscotland.scot/publications/nhs-waiting-times-diagnostics/diagnostic-waiting-times-waits-for-key-diagnostic-tests/)
  and accompanying data quality paper (https://publichealthscotland.scot/publications/diagnostic-waiting-times-data-supply-and-data-quality/).
  "\r\n</p>
