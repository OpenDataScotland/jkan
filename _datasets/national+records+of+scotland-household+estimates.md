notes: '<p>This dataset contains information on the number (''count'') of dwellings
  in each data zone and council area in Scotland, and the percentages (''ratio'')
  that are occupied, vacant, unoccupied and exempt from paying council tax, long-term
  empty, second homes, are occupied and exempt from paying council tax, and that are
  receiving a ''single adult'' council tax discount (i.e. dwellings subject to a Council
  Tax discount of 25 per cent). <br/><br/>The estimates are based on two Council Tax
  data collections carried out each year in September. Council Tax data contains information
  on the various discounts/exemptions awarded to each dwelling in Scotland. From these
  we can determine which dwellings are occupied and which are vacant or second homes.
  Council area and Scotland level estimates are produced using data provided by each
  council to the Scottish Government, using the Council Tax Base form, ''Ctaxbase'',
  available from the Scottish Government website. Data zone level estimates are produced
  using data provided by each council to National Records of Scotland (NRS) at data
  zone or postcode level. Data zone level estimates are constrained so that they sum
  to the Council Tax Base totals for a council area, unless a council has advised
  otherwise. For full details of the methods used see the ''Estimates of Households
  and Dwellings in Scotland'' publications on the <a href="https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/publications/households-and-dwellings-in-scotland-2024/#">NRS
  website</a>.<br/><br/>The number of occupied dwellings is roughly equivalent to
  the number of households in an area, which is why we refer to this dataset as ''Household
  Estimates''. This dataset provides the best source of information on the number
  of occupied dwellings or households at data zone level each year. However, for information
  on numbers of households in council areas and Scotland, separate mid-year household
  estimates are available on <a href="https://statistics.gov.scot/resource?uri=http%3A%2F%2Fstatistics.gov.scot%2Fdata%2Fmid-year-household-estimates">statistics.gov.scot</a>
  and the <a href="https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/publications/households-and-dwellings-in-scotland-2024/#">NRS
  website</a>. These are based on the September occupied dwellings data but are adjusted
  (i) to estimate number of households in June, (ii) to account for the estimated
  number of occupied dwellings which contain more than one household and (iii) to
  account for the estimated number of communal establishments which have been included
  in Council Tax records.<br/><br/>Additional summary statistics for National Parks,
  Strategic Development Plan areas, urban-rural classifications and Scottish Index
  of Multiple Deprivation deciles are published annually in ''Estimates of Households
  and Dwellings in Scotland'' which is available from the <a href="https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/publications/small-area-statistics-on-households-and-dwellings-2024/#">NRS
  website</a>.<br/><br/>The estimated total number of dwellings in this dataset differ
  from those in the <a href="https://statistics.gov.scot/resource?uri=http%3A%2F%2Fstatistics.gov.scot%2Fdata%2Fdwellings-by-council-tax-band-summary-current-geographic-boundaries">Dwellings
  by Council Tax Band Summary</a>, <a href="https://statistics.gov.scot/data/dwellings-rooms">Dwellings
  by Number of Rooms</a>, <a href="https://statistics.gov.scot/data/dwellings-median-number-of-rooms">Dwellings
  Median Number of Rooms</a> and <a href="https://statistics.gov.scot/data/dwellings-type">Dwellings
  by Type</a> datasets as these are extracted from a different source (the Assessors''
  Portal) at a different time of year (December, or January the following year). <br/><br/><br/><em>Total
  Dwellings</em>  A ''dwelling'' refers to the accommodation itself, for example a
  house or a flat, and includes second homes that are not let out commercially. Caravans
  count as dwellings if they are someone''s main house.<br/><br/><em>Which Are Occupied</em>  Any
  dwelling apart from those which are vacant or second homes. The number of occupied
  dwellings is a good estimate of the number of households.<br/><br/><em>Which Are
  Vacant</em>  This includes dwellings that are exempt from Council Tax and are unoccupied;
  and dwellings which are recorded on Council Tax systems as being long-term empty
  properties.<br/><br/><em>With ''Unoccupied Exemptions''</em>  Dwellings exempt from
  Council Tax, which are unoccupied, such as new dwellings, those undergoing repair
  or awaiting demolition and dwellings where the previous owner has died.<br/><br/><em>Which
  are Long-term empty</em>  Dwellings which are empty properties. This should include
  properties subject to discounts and levies due to be empty except the properties
  recorded as Unoccupied dwellings exempt from paying Council Tax. ''Long-term empty''
  properties in these statistics nominally relate to those empty for 6 months or more.
  It was discovered that prior to 2024 some councils had also included within their
  reported data for this category properties empty for less than 6 months. The number
  of long-term (6 months or more) empty properties will therefore have been overstated
  in such instances. The guidance for the September 2024 data collection was strengthened
  to ensure that councils excluded properties empty for less than 6 months from this
  category in their data returns, This is reflected in the 2024 statistics in the
  CTax Base figures published by Scottish Government Local Government Finance Statistics,
  and also in the council level statistics for 2024 on long-term empty properties
  included in the Data file (tables 4 and 6c) of the <a href="https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/publications/households-and-dwellings-in-scotland-2024/#">Households
  and Dwellings 2024 publication</a>. However, not all councils were able to adjust
  the reporting software used to provide their small area data on the long-term empty
  category to similarly exclude properties empty for less than 6 months. As a result
  the statistics on ''long-term empty'' properties for Data Zones, and for the other
  geographies created as aggregations of Data Zones, will continue to have been overstated
  in such instances. Summing the number of ''long-term empty'' properties across all
  Data Zones in a council area will therefore not necessarily agree with the figure
  given for the council in tables 4 and 6c of the main publication.<br/><br/><em>Which
  Are Second Homes</em>   These are dwellings that are not someone''s main residence
  and that are occupied for at least 25 days a year. These include self-catering holiday
  accommodations available to let for a total of less than 140 days per year. Second
  homes which are let out for 140 days or more are not included in these figures as
  they are classed as business so pay non-domestic rates rather than Council Tax.
  Each council has discretion to apply a discount of between 10% and 50% on second
  homes, or may choose to apply no discount.<br/><br/><em>With ''Occupied Exemptions''</em>  Dwellings
  exempt from Council Tax, which are occupied. This includes: dwellings only occupied
  by students, armed forces accommodation owned by the Secretary of State for Defence,
  dwellings which are the sole residence only of people aged under 18 or people who
  are classed as severely mentally impaired, trial flats used by registered housing
  associations, and prisons.<br/><br/><br/><em>With Single Adult Discount</em>  Dwellings
  subject to a Council Tax discount of 25 per cent. This may include, for example,
  dwellings with a single adult, dwellings with one adult living with one or more
  children, or with one or more adults who are ''disregarded'' for Council Tax purposes.<br/><br/>For
  a short explanation of the categories:<br/><br/><em>Total Dwellings</em>  =   <em>Which
  Are Occupied</em>  + <em>Which Are Vacant</em>  + <em>Which Are Second Homes</em><br/>  <br/><em>Which
  Are Vacant</em>   =    <em>With ''Unoccupied Exemptions''</em>  +  <em>Which are
  Long-term empty</em><br/><br/><em>With ''Occupied Exemptions''</em>    and <em>With
  Single Adult Discount</em>    are a fraction of  <em>Total Dwellings</em><br/><br/>Because
  figures have been rounded to the nearest whole number, totals might be not equal
  to the sum of  categories.<br/><br/>Due to the methodology used, totals for Council
  are not equal to the sum of Data Zone values.<br/><br/><em>NOTE: 12th January 2026
  - Minor corrections have been made to the values for the number/percentage of vacant
  dwellings to fix an error in the code used to calculate them. All other numbers
  in the data set are unaffected by these corrections.</em></p>'
