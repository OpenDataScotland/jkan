  is defined as a young person of school leaving age who left school (from S4, S5
  or S6) during or at the end of the school year.<br/><br/>School leavers are recorded
  against the school at which they were present in the pupil census in September.
  Young people who move to another school or outwith Scotland are not included.<br/><br/>For
  some data points, a special character is provided instead of a number. These have
  the meaning:<br/>   <br/> * 'NA' - data not available<br/> * '*' - the value has
  been hidden for disclosure reasons<br/> * '#' - data not available because the school
  is a junior high school with no year S5 or S6</p>
