notes: <p>Information is taken from attendance returns linked to the September Scottish
  Pupil Census, for the relevant year, for publicly funded schools. <br/><br/>The
  data does not include <br/><br/><em> pupils attending special schools;<br/></em>
  pupils attending independent schools;<br/><em> pupils educated outwith the school
  education system (for example at home);<br/></em> adults attending publicly funded
  secondary schools.<br/><br/>The data was collected through the Scottish Exchange
  of Educational Data (ScotXed) project. More information on the ScotXed project is
  available on <a href="http://www.gov.scot/Topics/Statistics/ScotXed">http://www.gov.scot/Topics/Statistics/ScotXed</a>.
  <br/><br/>A range of information was collected for each individual pupil, including
  the pupil home postcode, gender and whether a pupil is registered for free school
  meals. Postcodes were submitted for about 99% of pupils in publicly funded primary
  and secondary schools. Results here do not include pupils with a missing or invalid
  postcode. Therefore, the national figures may not be the same as national figures
  published elsewhere. <br/><br/>The data covers the whole academic year. In some
  authorities schools did not receive full instructions from their local authority
  about new definitions for attendance for 2003/04, which may affect the comparability
  of the data for that year. While information is received at pupil level, there is
  currently no unique pupil identifier for tracking pupils who move school to another
  authority. This means that where a pupil has moved schools, only the attendance
  record from the original school has been used. It was possible to link about 95
  per cent of possible attendance.</p>
