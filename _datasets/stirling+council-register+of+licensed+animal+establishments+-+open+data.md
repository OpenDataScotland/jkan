notes: '<div><b>The Animal Welfare (licensing of Activities Involving Animals) (Scotland)
  Regulations 2021</b></div>

  <div><br /></div>

  <div>Licences issued under The Animal Welfare (licensing of Activities Involving
  Animals) (Scotland) Regulations 2021 are required to be kept in a public register.
  The register can be viewed and contains details of all current animal licences issued
  by Stirling Council.</div>

  <div><br /></div>

  <div>The Regulations came into force on 1 September 2021. They replaced previous
  legislation covering pet sales and dog breeding and introduced new licensing requirements
  for cat and rabbit breeders, animal welfare establishments (such as animal sanctuaries
  and rehoming centres) and other pet rehoming activities, subject to operators meeting
  the licensing criteria for each activity.</div>
