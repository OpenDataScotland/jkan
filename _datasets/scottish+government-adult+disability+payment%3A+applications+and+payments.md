notes: <p>Adult Disability Payment is the replacement for Personal Independence Payment
  and Working Age Disability Living Allowance in Scotland, which were previously delivered
  by the Department for Work and Pensions.<br/><br/>It is the second application-based
  disability benefit to be introduced by the Scottish Government after Child Disability
  Payment and is administered by Social Security Scotland. It provides money to help
  with the extra daily living and mobility costs that a person living with a disability
  or long term health condition might have.<br/><br/>From the 21 March 2022, new applications
  were taken for Adult Disability Payment for adults living in the pilot areas of
  Dundee City, Na h-Eileanan Siar and Perth and <br/>Kinross. The pilot expanded to
  include Angus, North Lanarkshire and South <br/>Lanarkshire on 20 June 2022. It
  further expanded to include Fife, Aberdeen City, <br/>Aberdeenshire, Moray, North
  Ayrshire, East Ayrshire and South Ayrshire on 25 July <br/>2022, before launching
  nationally to all remaining local authorities on 29 August 2022. <br/>This dataset
  includes received applications (i.e. the total Part 1 applications registered),
  authorised applications and the total value of issued payments for Adult Disability
  Payment. Statistics are provided for the last day of the month (e.g. figures for
  2023-04 are for applications received or authorised as of 30 April 2023).<br/><br/>Further
  information on new applications and clients who have had their awards transferred,
  award types, processing times and re-determinations can be found in the associated
  <a href="https://www.socialsecurity.gov.scot/reporting/publications/adult-disability-payment-statistics-to-30-april-2025">publication
  page</a>.<br/></p>
