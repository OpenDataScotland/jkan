notes: '<p><strong>Overview</strong></p>

  <p>This dataset provides detailed information on civic licences regulated under
  the Civic Government (Scotland) Act 1982, maintained by Stirling Council. It excludes
  licensed premises and focuses on the licensing and regulation of various activities
  across the Stirling Council area in Scotland.</p>

  <p><strong>Data Contents</strong></p>

  <ul><li>Licence details including type and reference number.</li><li>Licence holder
  information and duration metrics such as date first granted, last renewal date,
  and next renewal date.</li><li>Information on goods and services provided, as well
  as the premises and areas covered by each licence.</li><li>Operating times and publication
  status of the licences.</li></ul>

  <p><strong>Geographic Coverage</strong></p>

  <p>The data specifically covers the jurisdiction of Stirling Council in Scotland,
  encompassing various licensed activities regulated locally.</p>

  <p><strong>Licensing and Terms</strong></p>

  <p>The dataset is published under the UK Open Government Licence and complies with
  various attribution requirements related to public sector and Ordnance Survey data,
  promoting open use with adherence to licensing terms.</p>

  <p><strong>Applications</strong></p>

  <ul><li>Useful for regulatory compliance, public transparency, and local government
  licensing management.</li><li>Valuable resource for businesses, researchers, and
  the general public interested in civic licensing activities within Stirling Council.</li></ul>'
