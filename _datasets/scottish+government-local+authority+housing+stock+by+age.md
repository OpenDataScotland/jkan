notes: <p>Local Authority stock refers to the number of dwellings owned by local authorities,
  whether or not they are occupied or currently available for letting. However it
  excludes dwellings that are currently out of use and expected to be out of use for
  at least a year.  Information is collected as at 31 March each year, as part of
  the Housing Statistics Annual Return. <br/>Data suppliers are asked to split stock
  figures into the age categories pre 1919, 1919-1944, 1945-1964, 1965-1982, post
  1982.<br/>Due to some known inconsistencies in the local authority stock dataset,
  the tables published on the open data platform exclude data relating to the years
  1998 to 2000. A full dataset is published on the <a href="http://www.gov.scot/Topics/Statistics/Browse/Housing-Regeneration/HSfS/StockPublicSector">housing
  statistics website here</a><br/><br/>Where a table shows missing values, no data
  for that year was supplied by the relevant local authority.</p>
