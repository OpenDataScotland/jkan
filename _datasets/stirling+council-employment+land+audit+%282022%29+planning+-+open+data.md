  \ policies and the strategies which underpin them.</div>\n<div><br /></div>\n<div>For\

  \ Industry) and 6 (Storage and Distribution) of the Town and Country Planning (Use\
  \ Classes) (Scotland) Order 1997. \u2018Employment Land\u2019 is defined as land\
  \ currently in use, designated in the LDP, or with planning permission for \u2018\
  employment\u2019 uses.\_</div>"
