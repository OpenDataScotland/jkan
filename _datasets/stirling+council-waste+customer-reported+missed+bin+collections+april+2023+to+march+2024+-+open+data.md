notes: '<p><span>This dataset provides information about customer-reported missed
  bin collections recorded by Stirling Council, including when each call was logged,
  its recorded status and, where applicable, when it was resolved.</span></p>

  <p><span>Each record represents a customer-reported missed colelction and includes
  the local authority name and code, associated street name and postcode, date logged,
  call status, resolution date, number of days active, resolution time category, reporting
  year and report type. A postcode match indicator records whether the associated
  postcode has been matched during data processing. Where a street name is unavailable,
  this is identified in the relevant field.</span></p>

  <p><span>For resolved calls, the time taken to resolve a call is calculated as the
  difference in calendar days between the date logged and the date resolved. A call
  logged and resolved on the same date is recorded as zero days; a call logged on
  3 July and resolved on 5 July is recorded as two days. This measure includes weekends
  and public holidays and does not represent the number of working days or staff hours
  spent handling a call.</span></p>'
