notes: <p>Annual publication of mid-year population estimates by age and sex as at
  30 June every year, where population for higher geographies is aggregated from the
  population estimates for 2011 Data Zones.<br/><br/><a href="http://statistics.gov.scot/data/population-estimates-historical-geographic-boundaries">Population
  estimates based on 2001 data zones</a> are also available, but are no longer updated.
  <br/><br/>Population estimates for mid-2012 to mid-2022 have been revised following
  Scotland's Census 2022. <br/><br/>Data zones are the small area geography used by
  the Scottish Government to allow statistics to be available across a number of policy
  areas. More information on data zone geography can be found on the <a href="http://www.gov.scot/Publications/2004/02/18917/33243">Scottish
  Government</a> website. There are 6,976 data zones in Scotland.<br/><br/>There are
  1,235 intermediate zones in Scotland. <a href="https://www.spatialdata.gov.scot/geonetwork/srv/api/records/389787c0-697d-4824-9ca9-9ce8cb79d6f5">More
  information on intermediate zones</a> can be found on the SpatialData.gov.scot Metadata
  Portal. <br/><br/>This data is also available by single year of age on the NRS website.  These
  breakdowns are available for <a href="https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/statistics-and-data/statistics/statistics-by-theme/population/population-estimates/mid-year-population-estimates">Scotland
  and its council areas</a>, as well as for <a href="https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/statistics-and-data/statistics/statistics-by-theme/population/population-estimates/2011-based-special-area-population-estimates/small-area-population-estimates">2011
  data zones</a> and <a href="https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/statistics-and-data/statistics/statistics-by-theme/population/population-estimates/2011-based-special-area-population-estimates">other
  geographies</a>.<br/><br/>The NRS website has <a href="https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/statistics-and-data/statistics/statistics-by-theme/population/population-estimates/mid-year-population-estimates">more
  information on population statistics</a>.</p>
