notes: "<p>The Input-Output framework of the <a href=\"http://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/esa-2010\"\
  >European System of Accounts (ESA 2010)</a> consists of three types of tables:<br/><br/><em>\
  \ Supply Tables,<br/></em> Use Tables and<br/><em> Symmetric Input-Output Tables<br/><br/>The\
  \ Tables provide a complete picture of the flows of goods and services (products)\
  \ in the Scottish onshore</em> economy for a given year. They detail the relationship\
  \ between producers and consumers and the interdependencies of industries.<br/><br/>Supply\
  \ and Use Tables are constructed directly from survey and other data sources. The\
  \ Supply Table provides estimates of the output of a large number of differentiated\
  \ products by each industry and the Use Table provides estimates of the inputs (of\
  \ products) used by each industry to produce their own output.<br/><br/>The Supply\
  \ and Use Tables are the basic building blocks; all other Input-Output analyses\
  \ are derived from them.<br/>Symmetric Tables (a.k.a. the Analytical Tables) represent\
  \ the modelling aspect of the Input-Output framework. The Scottish Government produces\
  \ annual Industry by Industry, Product by Product and Leontief Inverse Analytical\
  \ Tables.<br/><br/>The datasets in this zipped archive are provided for machine\
  \ readable applications. They include the:<br/><br/><em> Domestic Output at Basic\
  \ Prices and Supply of Products at Purchasers\xE2\u20AC\u2122 Prices Table<br/></em>\
  \ Combined Use Table in Purchasers' Prices<br/><em> Domestic Industry by Industry\
  \ Table in Basic Prices<br/></em> Domestic Product by Product Table in Basic Prices<br/><em>\
  \ Leontief Type 1 and Type 2 matrices<br/></em> Output, Income, Employment and GVA\
  \ Multipliers and Effects<br/><em> GVA at current basic prices and GVA weights by\
  \ industry tables<br/></em> Imports and Exports detail tables<br/><br/>All cash\
  \ values in these datasets are in current price \xC2\xA3m.<br/><br/>For details\
  \ on the contents of this data collection, see _MetaData.xlsx in the zip file, or\
  \ visit <a href=\"https://www.gov.scot/input-output\">www.gov.scot/input-output</a>\
  \ for more information. <br/><br/>For guidance on how to access this zipped collection\
  \ of files using our API, please <a href=\"https://guides.statistics.gov.scot/article/41-api-access-for-file-datasets-not-linked-data\"\
  >visit our help guide</a>.<br/><br/>*Satellite accounts for extra-regio activities\
  \ are available as statistics in development as part of the <a href=\"https://www.gov.scot/collections/economy-statistics/#thescottishnationalaccountsprogramme(snap)\"\
  >Scottish National Accounts Programme (SNAP)</a></p>"
