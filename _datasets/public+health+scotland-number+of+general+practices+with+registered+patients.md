  Health Scotland Open Data</a> platform for the relevant data.<br/><br/>Data indicates
  the number of general practices (GP surgeries) by area as at 1 October for each
  year presented. <br/><br/>Since the introduction of the new GP contract (the General
  Medical Services contract) on 1 April 2004, patients register with a practice rather
  than a specific GP. <br/><br/>Please note that each practice has been allocated
  to one area based on the postcode of its main premises. However, practices may also
  operate branch surgeries from other locations and these locations may sit within
  a different area to that of the main practice premises. Practices in one area may
  provide care for patients living in another area. This is particularly likely where
  practices are located close to a boundary between areas, and/or where a practice
  operates one or more branch surgeries. <br/><br/>The practice counts are based on
  regular GP surgeries that have at least one contracted GP partner or salaried GP
  working at them. They exclude practices which do not have registered patients and
  which are run directly by the NHS Boards e.g. Out of Hours Services, virtual clinics.
  <br/><br/>Data Source for General Practices: General Practitioner Contractor Database
  (GPCD), Information Services Division, NHS National Services Scotland. <br/><br/>Further
  information on GP workforce and practice populations in Scotland is available on

  Website</a></p>'
