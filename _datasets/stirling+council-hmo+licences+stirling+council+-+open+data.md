  which is sent to all Local Authorities. The data collected asks local authorities
  to give the figures of Total HMO Licences in Force at March 31st of each year. Licenses
  in force will exclude withdrawn applications and include applications refused and
  subsequently appealed. They also exclude licences which are currently in the process
  of being renewed.
