notes: '<p>Note: This data will not be receiving updates through this portal. <br/><br/>Number,
  and percent, of children who have completed their primary immunisation course of
  the 6-in-1 vaccine by 24 months of age. <br/><br/>From 1 October 2017, the 6-in-1
  vaccine, which also protects against Hepatitis B, replaced the 5-in-1 vaccine in
  the routine childhood immunisation schedule.  <br/><br/>The 6-in-1 vaccine protects
  against diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis, polio, Haemophilus influenzae type b (Hib)
  and Hepatitis B.  A complete primary course by 24 months of age is 3 doses of vaccine.<br/><br/>Each
  cohort reported consists of children reaching 24 months of age in the calendar year.<br/><br/>The
  NHS Board and local authority are derived from child''s home postcode and therefore
  data with invalid or missing postcodes are not included in the NHS Board and local
  authority totals.  As a result the numbers and rates often differ from those published
  in the ISD National Statistics publication which can be accessed from the <a href="https://www.isdscotland.org/Health-Topics/Child-Health/Publications/">NHS
  Information Services Division website</a>. In the publication the NHS Board of residence
  figures are based on the NHS Board recorded on the Scottish Immunisation and Recall
  System (SIRS) and the local authority figures are based on the local authority of
  the GP practice  where the child is registered. <br/><br/><br/><br/><br/>Source:
  Scottish Immunisation and Recall System (SIRS), ISD Scotland.</p>'
