  zones using a population weighted average. The datazones are then ordered by quintile
  and decile, from least to most accessible.<br/><br/>The annual report can be accessed
  at the <a href="https://www.transport.gov.scot/our-approach/statistics#42764">Transport
  Scotland website</a>.</p>
