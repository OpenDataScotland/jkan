  \ Census and were updated by taking account of births, deaths and migration up to\
  \ 30 June 2024. Numbers of births and deaths are obtained from the civil registration\
  \ system.The latest international migration estimates use different data sources\
  \ and methods for each nationality grouping:</span></p>\n<ul>\n <li><span style='font-size:\
  \ 16px;'>Non-EU nationals: Estimates are based on the Home Office Borders and Immigration\
  \ data, which combine visa</span><br /><span style='font-size: 16px;'>and travel\
  \ information, to link an individual's travel movements into and out of the country.</span></li>\n\
  \ <li><span style='font-size: 16px;'>EU nationals: Estimates are largely based on\
  \ administrative data using the Registration and Population</span><br /><span style='font-size:\
  \ 16px;'>Interaction Database (RAPID). Some adjustments are made using information\
  \ from the International Passenger</span><br /><span style='font-size: 16px;'>Survey\
  \ (IPS).</span></li>\n <li><span style='font-size: 16px;'>British nationals: The\
  \ IPS is the main source of information. This is combined with 2022 Census data\
  \ on</span><br /><span style='font-size: 16px;'>the age and sex distribution of\
  \ British passport holders to disaggregate migration estimates to council areas.</span></li>\n\
  \ </ul>\n<p><span style='font-size: 16px;'>Migration within the UK is based on the\
  \ National Health Service Central Register (NHSCR) and the Community Health Index</span><br\
  \ /><span style='font-size: 16px;'>(CHI). </span><span style='word-spacing: normal;'>Armed\
  \ forces data comes from the 2022 Census and from the Ministry of Defence. Data\
  \ on the prisoner population in Scotland is obtained from Justice Analytical Services\
  \ in the Scottish Government.</span></p>\n<p><span style='word-spacing: normal;'>Data\
  \ collection occurred throughout the mid-year to mid-year period:</span></p>\n<ul>\n\

  \ <li><span style='word-spacing: normal;'>Armed Forces &ndash; April 2025.</span></li>\n\
  \ <li><span style='word-spacing: normal;'>The international migration data was finalised\
