notes: '<div>How demand-responsive transport works;</div>

  <div><br /></div>

  <div>Demand-responsive transport (DRT) is system that operates in areas where there
  are no regular bus routes. It works like a taxi service and costs around the same
  as a bus fare.</div>

  <div><br /></div>

  <div>DRT is for anyone who wants to travel in rural parts of the Stirling Council
  area, regardless of</div>

  <div><ul><li>age</li><li>disability</li><li>whether they''re a resident or a visitor</li></ul></div>

  <div>If you use DRT, you might share transport with other travellers who are making
  the same or a similar journey.</div>

  <div><br /></div>

  <div>When DRT is available?</div>

  <div>DRT is available all year round, apart from:</div>

  <div><ul><li>25 December</li><li>26 December</li><li>1 January</li><li>2 January</li></ul></div>

  <div>All DRT schemes are available from Monday to Friday. However, some services
  are not available on other days.</div>

  <div><br /></div>

  <div>DRT Services that are not available on Saturdays and Sundays:</div>

  <div>On Saturdays, there is no DRT service in the following areas:</div>

  <div><ul><li>Croftamie and Drymen</li><li>Fintry</li></ul>On Sundays, there is no
  DRT service in the following areas:</div>

  <div><ul><li>Croftamie and Drymen</li><li>Fintry</li><li>Killin and Strathfillan</li></ul></div>'
