  \ Local Development Plans (LDPs) to protect land for business, industrial, and other\
  \ employment-generating uses. These areas are identified based on economic need,\
  \ demand for employment land, and their suitability for various industries.\_<div><br\

  \ economic growth, attract investment, and prevent the loss of strategically important\
  \ land to competing uses such as housing. Policies governing these areas ensure\
  \ that development proposals align with economic objectives while balancing local\
  \ land use pressures.\_</div><div><br /></div><div>Decisions on employment safeguard\
  \ areas are guided by national planning policies, economic strategies, and local\
  \ priorities to maintain a diverse and resilient economy.</div></p>"
