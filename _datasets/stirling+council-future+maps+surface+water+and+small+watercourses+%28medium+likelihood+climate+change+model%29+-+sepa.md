notes: '<p><strong>Future Maps - Climate Change Model</strong></p>

  <p>The following probabilities are available for surface water &amp; small watercourse
  flooding:&nbsp;</p>

  <ul><li>High - 10 year return period,&nbsp;</li><li>Medium - 200 year return period,&nbsp;</li><li>Low
  - 1000 year return period, and the 200 year return period plus climate change using
  rainfall uplifts for 2070 from the FUTURE-DRAINAGE project.&nbsp;</li></ul>

  <p>The FUTURE-DRAINAGE project is based on analysis of the UK Climate Projections
  2018 (UKCP18) high resolution (UKCP Local) projections for Representative Concentration
  Pathway 8.5 (RCP8.5).&nbsp;</p>

  <p>The climate scenario has been defined by the 50th percentile for 2070. The surface
  water and small watercourses hazard maps show (where available): Flood extent, flood
  depth and flood velocities (where appropriate).</p>
