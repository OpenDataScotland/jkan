notes: <p>Scottish Child Payment has been introduced for low-income families with
  children aged under 6. It is intended to provide regular, additional financial support
  for families already in receipt of qualifying benefits to assist with the costs
  of caring for a child.<br/><br/>Social Security Scotland invited clients to apply
  from 9 November 2020 in advance of the benefit being launched on 15 February 2021
  to help manage the expected demand. Applicants began to receive a decision from
  15 February onwards, with the first payments made to clients from the end of February
  2021.<br/><br/>Scottish Child Payment was extended to older children aged under
  16 on 14 November 2022.<br/><br/>This dataset includes received applications, authorised
  applications and issued payments for Scottish Child Benefit. Statistics are provided
  for the last day of the month (e.g. figures for 2020-11 are for applications received
  or authorised as of 30 November 2020) and the financial year (e.g. figures for 2020/2021
  are for applications received or authorised in between April 2020 and March 2021).<br/><br/>Further
  information on application, processing times, re-determinations and appeals can
  be found in the associated <a href="https://www.socialsecurity.gov.scot/publications/2026/02/scottish-child-payment-to-31-march-2026">publication</a>.</p>
