notes: <p>Consistent, reliable biodiversity information about development sites across
  Scotland.   These assessments are commissioned from AI Dash by developers to understand
  the biodiversity characteristics of their sites, and to inform development planning.  Assessment
  outputs are then made available to local planning authorities, enabling them to
  access consistent biodiversity information without needing to commission their own
  underlying assessments.  This dataset comprises three layers - points (recording
  individual features such as trees), lines (recording linear features such as hedgerows
  and watercourses), and polygons (recording larger areas of habitat as well as site
  and/or study area boundaries).  The dataset will be updated as and when new survey
  data is created and provided.</p>
