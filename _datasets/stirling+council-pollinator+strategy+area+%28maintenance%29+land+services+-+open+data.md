notes: "<p>Land Services carries out grass cutting operations across the Stirling\
  \ Council area. This dataset details information regarding both open space maintenance\
  \ and those areas identified, and managed, as part of Stirling Council's Pollinator\
  \ Strategy.<div><br /></div><div><div>Improved greenspaces are vital for people\
  \ to bring them closer to nature, and provide physical and mental well-being benefits.\
  \ Stirling Council\u2019s Pollinator Strategy 2023 \u2013 2028 has been approved\
  \ by the Environment, Transport and Net Zero Committee as a pillar of Stirling\u2019\
  s attempts to tackle the global climate crisis, providing higher quality greenspaces\
  \ across Stirling.</div><div><br /></div><div>Insects such as bees, butterflies\
  \ and beetles are expected to thrive as a result of changes to the Council\u2019\
  s grassland management.</div><div><br /></div><div><div>Whilst the majority of the\
  \ grass areas Stirling Council maintains is owned by the authority, a large portion\
  \ of grass cutting operations currently maintain areas Stirling Council has no responsibility\
  \ for. Such areas are under review.</div><div><br /></div><div><div>The dataset's\
  \ schema includes;</div><div><ul><li>Site name, locality, and town information</li><li>Feature\
  \ (Grass) Type and Maintenance descriptions.</li><li>Feature Group - whether the\
  \ area is managed as open space or as a pollinator site.</li><li>Council Title information\
  \ - if an area intersects with information within our Title Deeds dataset, this\
  \ will be shown here.</li><li>Community Council and Ward information.</li><li>Postcode\
  \ - predominate postcode of an area (some areas straddle multiple postcodes but\
  \ only one is shown)</li></ul><div>Please Note: the council title deed information\
  \ is just for reference and should not be used as a definitive record for an area.</div></div></div></div></div></p>"
