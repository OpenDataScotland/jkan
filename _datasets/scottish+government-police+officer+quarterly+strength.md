notes: <p>The first publication of Police Officer Quarterly Strength statistics as
  Official Statistics was on 2 December 2008. Prior to this publication, figures were
  collated by the Scottish Government and released through responses to Parliamentary
  Questions and by depositing the figures in the Scottish Parliamentary Library.<br/><br/>The
  Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 created Police Scotland and the Scottish
  Police Authority (SPA), which came into being on 1 April 2013. <br/>The statistics
  in this release are based upon quarterly returns made by Police Scotland to the
  Scottish Government, and are a by-product of administrative processes, generated
  directly from the HR systems. The published police officer figures are sourced from
  Police Scotland's personnel records and as such the quality and reliability is good.
  <br/><br/>Figures include all police officers appointed by the Chief Constable of
  Police Scotland and senior police officers appointed by the Scottish Police Authority,
  excluding those on career breaks. Deployment of police officers is a matter for
  the Chief Constable of Police Scotland.<br/><br/>The following definitions apply
  to police officers in Scotland. These statistics include all officers deployed with
  the 13 local policing divisions, specialist and operational support, central functions,
  officers undertaking Scottish Police College training, long-term sick leave and
  maternity/adoption leave, officers at ports and airports paid either wholly or partially
  by the port/airport authority. Additional officers either wholly or partially funded
  by the local authority or an external body, and all officers otherwise seconded
  to Scottish Government, His Majesty's Inspectorate of Constabulary for Scotland
  (HMICS), Scottish Police Authority and elsewhere. <br/><br/>These figures do not
  include police officers employed in Scotland by the British Transport Police, Ministry
  of Defence Police or the Civil Nuclear Constabulary.<br/><br/>Police Scotland publish
  a <a href="https://www.scotland.police.uk/about-us/how-we-do-it/police-scotland-officer-numbers/">geographical
  break down of police officers</a> on the Police Scotland website.<br/><br/>Further
  information on <a href="https://www.gov.scot/collections/crime-and-justice-statistics/">Crime
  and Justice statistics within Scotland</a> <br/> can be accessed on the Scottish
  Government website.</p>
