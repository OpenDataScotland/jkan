notes: <p>Community payback orders (CPOs) replaced probation, community service and
  supervised attendance orders. They were introduced for offences committed on or
  after 1st February 2011. CPOs are now by far the most commonly issued social work
  order.<br/>This data is derived from an annual return made to the Scottish Government
  by local authority social work departments and gives the number of CPOs commenced
  in each year from 2011-12 by age group and gender.<br/>More data which shows the
  number of different social work order types (encompassing drug treatment &amp; testing
  orders and fiscal work orders as well as CPOs and their predecessors) back to 2004-05
  can be found in the datasets <a href="http://statistics.gov.scot/data/social-work-orders-commenced">Social
  Work Orders Commenced</a> and <a href="http://statistics.gov.scot/data/social-work-orders-terminated">Social
  Work Orders Terminated</a> on this website. Further data on CPOs at local authority
  level can be found on the Scottish Government <a href="https://www.gov.scot/collections/criminal-justice-social-work/">website</a>.</p>
