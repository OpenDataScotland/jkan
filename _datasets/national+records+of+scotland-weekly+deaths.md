notes: '<p>This dataset presents the weekly number of all deaths, deaths involving
  COVID-19, and deaths involving influenza registered in Scotland. Tables for this
  publication are also available on the <a href="https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/publications/deaths-registered-weekly-in-scotland">NRS
  website</a>. Data for 2026 are provisional.</p>

  <p>Figures are based on date of registration. In Scotland deaths must be registered
  within 8 days although in practice, the average time between death and registration
  is around 3 days. See the <a href="https://webarchive.nrscotland.gov.uk/20240326182002/https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk/statistics-and-data/statistics/statistics-by-theme/vital-events/general-background-information/births-and-deaths-days-until-registration">NRS
  website</a> for more information on days between occurrence and registration.</p>

  <p>Figures for deaths involving COVID-19 and deaths involving influenza are provided
  on two bases:</p>

  <p><em>underlying cause</em> -cases where it was the disease or injury which initiated
  the chain of morbid events leading directly to death, or was the accident/act which
  produced the fatal injury; </p>

  <p><em>any mention</em> -i.e. whether it appeared to be the underlying cause of
  the death, or was just a factor which contributed to, or may have hastened, the
  occurrence of the death. </p>

  <p>Every death has just one <em>underlying cause</em> code, and so is counted only
  once in figures which are produced on the <em>underlying cause</em> basis. A death
  may have several other causes coded as contributory factors, so could be counted
  several times in figures which are produced on the <em>any mention</em> base. </p>

  <p>Deaths involving COVID-19 include any deaths where COVID-19 is mentioned on the
  death certificate, whether it is the underlying cause of death or a contributory
  condition. The ICD-10 codes U07.1, U07.2, U09.9 or U10.9 are used to identify deaths
  which involve COVID -19. </p>

  <p>The ICD-10 codes J09 - J11 are used to identify deaths which involve influenza.
  Caution is advised when interpreting trends over time. Influenza may be under-documented
  as an underlying or contributory cause of death on certificates, and the degree
  to which this occurs may vary over time. It can be influenced by things such as
  the availability of testing, and the awareness of certifying doctors regarding current
  levels of infection/epidemics. More information can be found in this paper: <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/7253967_The_impact_of_introducing_ICD-10_on_analysis_of_respiratory_mortality_trends_in_England_and_Wales">The
  impact of introducing ICD-10 on analysis of respiratory mortality trends in England
  and Wales</a></p>

  <p>Data on deaths involving COVID-19 broken down by age and administrative area
  can be accessed from <a href="http://statistics.gov.scot/data/deaths-involving-coronavirus-covid-19">Deaths
  involving COVID-19 on statistics.gov,scot</a> . Since March 2026 we stopped publishing
  these breakdowns.</p>'
