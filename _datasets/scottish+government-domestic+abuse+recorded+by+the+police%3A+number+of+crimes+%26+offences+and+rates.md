notes: "<p>Official Statistics on the number of domestic abuse aggravated crimes and\
  \ offences, recorded by Police Scotland. The full publication is available on the\
  \ <a href=\"https://www.gov.scot/collections/domestic-abuse-in-scotland-statistics/\"\
  >Scottish Government website</a>.</p>\n<p>This data covers crimes and offences recorded\
  \ over 12 months between 1 April to 31 March, for 2024-25.</p>\n<p>Statistics provided\
  \ here present the number of domestic abuse aggravated crimes and offences recorded\
  \ by the police and rates per 10,000 population. The rates of crimes and offences\
  \ per 10,000 population are also presented at a Local Authority level.</p>\n<p>Domestic\
  \ abuse recorded by the police does not reveal the incidence of all domestic abuse\
  \ committed in Scotland, as not all incidents are reported to the police.</p>\n\
  <p>The definition of domestic abuse used by Police Scotland is: \u2018Any form of\
  \ physical, verbal, sexual, psychological or financial abuse which might amount\
  \ to criminal conduct and which takes place within the context of a relationship.\
  \ The relationship will be between partners (married, cohabiting, civil partnership\
  \ or otherwise) or ex-partners. The abuse can be committed in the home or elsewhere\
  \ including online\u2019.</p>\n<p>Contraventions of Scottish criminal law are divided\
  \ into crimes and offences. The term \u201Ccrime\" is generally used for the more\
  \ serious criminal acts and the less serious are termed \"offences\". The distinction\
  \ is made only for statistical reporting purposes and does not influence the way\
  \ the police investigate reports of criminal activity. The seriousness of the offence\
  \ is generally related to the maximum sentence that can be imposed and does not\
  \ relate to the impact on the individual experiencing the incident.</p>\n<p>From\
  \ 2024-25, domestic abuse aggravated crimes and offences are now recorded on Police\
  \ Scotland\u2019s new crime management system (UNIFI), instead of the iVPD. Given\
  \ this, it is no longer possible to report on the proportion of domestic abuse incidents\
  \ that involved a crime or offence. Furthermore, it is not possible to provide a\
  \ like-for-like comparison with previous years.</p>"
