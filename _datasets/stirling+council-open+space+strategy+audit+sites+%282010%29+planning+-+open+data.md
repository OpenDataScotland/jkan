notes: "<div>Stirling Council's Open Space Strategy 2011 set out a framework for the\
  \ protection, enhancement, and sustainable management of open spaces across the\
  \ council area. The strategy aimed to ensure that residents and visitors had access\
  \ to high-quality green spaces, recognising their importance for recreation, biodiversity,\
  \ and community well-being. It identified key challenges, such as maintaining and\
  \ improving existing spaces, ensuring new developments incorporated adequate open\
  \ space, and addressing issues of accessibility and connectivity.\_</div>\n<div><br\
  \ /></div>\n<div>The strategy also emphasised the role of open spaces in supporting\
  \ environmental sustainability, including flood management and climate adaptation.\
  \ Through a combination of policy recommendations and action plans, the 2011 Open\
  \ Space Strategy sought to guide future investment and planning decisions to create\
  \ a well-connected and diverse network of open spaces that met the needs of Stirling\u2019\
  s growing population.</div>\n<div><br /></div>\n<div><a href='https://www.stirling.gov.uk/media/cqrpdrgz/open-space-strategy.pdf'\
